# Jonathan Sánchez

**Backend Lead & Engineering Manager.** I lead a backend team of more than six engineers and
still design the systems we ship. Six years in, across two domains: edtech first, then
regulated fintech.

## Leading the team

- **Measure delivery, not activity.** DORA metrics through Swarmia: how often we ship, how
  long a change takes to get out, how often it breaks, how fast we recover.
- **What I deliberately do not measure**: hours logged, who is online, time at a desk. That is
  noise, and measuring it teaches a team to optimise the wrong thing.
- **Decisions get written down.** RFCs and PRDs before the code, so the argument happens once
  and the reasoning outlives the people who had it.
- **Technical debt is a line in the roadmap**, not a complaint in a retro. It is written down,
  prioritised against features, and paid on purpose.
- **Review at scale.** I automate the mechanical half of code review with AI so the team
  spends its attention on design instead of on style.
- The rest of the job: planning and roadmap, performance, error monitoring, and keeping the
  bug funnel from quietly becoming a second backlog.

## Building the system

- **Fintech** — Python, FastAPI, Django, microservices. I started in compliance and KYC and now
  work across the platform: transfer agency, portfolio management, reporting, and Odoo.
- **Edtech** — PHP and Yii2, Angular, Firebase, and a great deal of third-party integration.
  Full stack, including a 7.4 → 8.2 migration and a role-based access control layer.

What I care about in both: the domain before the framework, explicit API contracts, and a test
suite you trust enough to deploy on a Friday. Software that is still cheap to change in year
three is the whole objective; everything above is in service of that.
